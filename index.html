Aditya Bikram Thakur

Math and computer science at Washington and Lee.

My story so far

I grew up in Pokhara, Nepal, under Machhapuchhre, the “fish tail” mountain.

Budhanilkantha School, Kathmandu: A Levels in Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science and Mathematics, with an A* in all four. Valedictorian. 1600 on the SAT.

August 2026, about 12,000 km later: Washington and Lee University in Lexington, Virginia. A math and computer science double major, on a full-ride scholarship.

Projects

DiatoMeter (September 2026 to now): with William & Mary’s Nano & Biomaterials Lab, measuring tiny glass algae shells; 136 shells measured in about 15 seconds. Visit DiatoMeter, source code.

Summer 2026, two projects of my own: walden.life, Thoreau’s Walden one day at a time; and SATitude, practice for the digital SAT.

Experience

2025, learning on my own: the Algorithms and Machine Learning specializations from Stanford, and Harvard’s CS50 AI (twelve projects).

Summer 2024, Incubate Nepal: frontend developer on Nepalingo, an open-source app for learning Nepal’s indigenous languages. I built the flashcards, daily quiz and activity cards, and merged 11 pull requests.

Summer 2023, Student Research Council Nepal: Lipi AI, an app that reads Tibetan inscriptions from a phone photo. I built the backend, splitting stacked letters so OCR can read them.
